Getting Started with Our Prediction Platform

Welcome! Our platform provides you with powerful tools to predict construction costs using advanced machine learning techniques. Here's a simple guide to get you started:

  • Registration: First, you'll need to register. Provide a valid email and mobile number for important updates and communications.
  • Activation: Once registered, an administrator will activate your account, granting you full access to the system.
  • Login: After activation, you can securely log in to our platform using your registered credentials.
  • Dataset Upload: You can upload your construction cost datasets. Ensure your data columns align with our system's requirements. For optimal performance, data should be in integer or float format.
  • Existing Data: We've included the Adacel Technologies Limited dataset as a sample for testing and exploration.
  • Add New Data: You have the flexibility to expand existing datasets by adding new entries directly through our Django application.
  • Data Preparation: Click on the "Data Preparation" button to initiate the data cleaning process. Our system will handle preprocessing steps automatically.
  • Cleaned Data & Visualizations: After preparation, the cleaned dataset will be presented along with relevant and insightful graphs to help you understand the data.

Explore the features and leverage the power of machine learning to gain valuable insights into construction cost predictions!
